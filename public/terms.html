Email Sender · CSI VIT TERMS OF USE

Use outreach thoughtfully.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Email Sender is a tool for authorized CSI VIT sponsorship outreach. By using it, you agree to send only relevant messages to contacts you are permitted to contact and to follow applicable Google, Gmail, and local requirements.

Your account and messages

You are responsible for the accuracy of contact details, message content, recipients, attachments, and sender profile you submit. Review personalized messages and recipient counts before sending. Each row is sent as a separate email from your connected Gmail account.

Permission to send

You authorize the app to use its Gmail send permission to send the messages you confirm, plus test messages you request. The app does not promise inbox delivery, and Gmail or another provider may reject or limit a send.

Usage limits

The app enforces up to 100 contacts per batch, 100 send attempts per user per UTC day, and 2,000 attempts per app per UTC month. Tests and retries count toward limits. Provider free-plan quotas may make the app temporarily unavailable.

Data and termination

Batch records and files expire after 30 days. You can delete stored campaign data or your account in the app. Access may be suspended if a Google connection is revoked, limits are reached, or the app is used outside its sponsorship-outreach purpose.

Service availability

The tool is provided for the team’s internal use and may change as provider quotas, OAuth requirements, or the team’s configuration change. Keep your own copy of any information you need beyond the stated retention period.
