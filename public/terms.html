Email Sender · CSI VIT

Use this service with care.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Email Sender is for approved CSI VIT sponsorship outreach. Send relevant messages only to people who gave you permission to contact them. Follow the rules that apply to Google, Gmail, and your location.

Your account and messages

You are responsible for the contact details, message, recipients, attachments, and sender details you submit. Check each personalized message and the recipient count before you send. The app sends one email for each row through your connected Gmail account.

Permission to send

You let the app use Gmail send access for messages you confirm and test messages you request. The app cannot confirm inbox delivery. Gmail or another provider can reject or limit a send.

Usage limits

Each batch can contain up to 100 nonempty rows. Each user can make up to 100 send attempts per UTC day. The app allows up to 2,000 attempts per UTC month. Tests and retries count toward these limits. Free-plan provider limits can pause the app.

Data and termination

Campaign records and files expire after 30 days. You can delete campaign data or your account in the app. The team may suspend access if you remove Google access, reach a limit, or use the app for another purpose.

Service availability

The team provides this tool for internal use. The tool may change when provider limits, Google access rules, or team settings change. Keep a copy of any information you need after the retention period.
