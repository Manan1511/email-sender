Email Sender helps CSI VIT members prepare and send sponsorship emails. This page explains what data the app stores and how it uses that data. Before public launch, the team will add a support email address here.

What the app stores

The app stores your Google sign-in details, sender name and phone number, templates, contact rows, personalized messages, attachment details and files, and results for each recipient. You must grant separate Google permission before the app can send through Gmail.

How Gmail access works

The app can send email through your connected Google account. It cannot read your inbox. Your Gmail address must match your sign-in account. A refresh token lets the app renew Gmail access. The server encrypts this token and uses it only to send email. When you disconnect Gmail, the app deletes the token. You can also remove access in your Google Account.

Who can see your data

Each user can access only their own workspace records and files. Server functions check recipients and send a separate Gmail message to each person. Supabase provides sign-in, the database, and private file storage. Netlify runs the website and server functions. Google handles sign-in and Gmail sending. Each service has its own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Contact rows, personalized messages, results, and related files are deleted after 30 days. Templates and profile details stay until you delete them or your account. In Account settings, you can delete campaign history, unused attachments, or your account. Deleting your account removes the app’s Gmail token, workspace records, and private files. Google may keep sent messages in the sender’s Sent folder.

Limits and delivery

App and provider limits can pause sending. A success means Gmail accepted the send request. It does not confirm inbox delivery. If the result is unclear, the app marks it for review. Check Sent before you send the message again.

Questions

For privacy requests, contact the CSI VIT team that runs this workspace. The team must add a monitored address before it invites more users.
